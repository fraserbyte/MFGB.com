About the Messerschmitt Foundation of Great Britain

The Messerschmitt Foundation of Great Britain exists to preserve, document, and celebrate the remarkable story of Messerschmitt microcars and Kabinenroller — the ingenious "cabin scooters" that returned affordable, weather-proof personal mobility to a recovering post-war Europe. Across Great Britain we work with owners, restorers, museums, and historians to keep this chapter of automotive history alive for new generations.

Our work spans vehicle verification — confirming provenance, chassis numbers, and original specification against archival records — alongside practical maintenance advice for the Fichtel & Sachs two-stroke engines, the sequential hand-shift gearboxes with their cockpit-side gear levers, and the aircraft-style acrylic canopies that make these machines so distinctive. We also organise and support community events, concours displays, technical workshops, and mentorship pairings between seasoned restorers and newcomers.

At the heart of our mission is the belief that preservation is a craft of shared knowledge. Every vehicle we verify, every blueprint we archive, and every mentor we train strengthens the living record of the work of Fritz Fend — a technical designer whose vision shaped microcar motoring across Europe.

Vehicle Preservation

Guidance and mentorship for the conservation, restoration, and correct original-specification rebuild of KR175, KR200, and Tg500 microcars.

Archive & Blueprint Vault

A growing technical archive of factory documentation, production records, and restoration reference material — the foundation for every verification.

Community Registry

A living registry of surviving vehicles, owners, and restorers across Great Britain — connecting enthusiasts and preserving ownership history.
